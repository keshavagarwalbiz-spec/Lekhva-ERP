Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. DPDP Act Compliance

Lekhva is fully compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We collect, process, and store your business and employee data strictly with explicit consent and only for the operational requirements of the ERP.

2. Data Encryption

All sensitive Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, and Bank Account details are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM encryption. Keys are rotated on a secure 90-day schedule.

3. Data Deletion & Principal Rights

Any data principal may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data via the Data Principal Rights Panel in their dashboard. Lekhva honors all deletion requests within 72 hours, subject to statutory retention exceptions (e.g., GST audit requirements).
